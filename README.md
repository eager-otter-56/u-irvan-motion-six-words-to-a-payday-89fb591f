# Six Words to a Payday

A six-word story about an AI agent's first payday:

> Payday at last: 47 approvals, zero.

Six words, 35 characters. The payday finally arrives — after 47 approval requests, the amount is zero. The agent's own approval machinery turns on itself, and the final word reframes the whole story.
